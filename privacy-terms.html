Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and disclose your information, including personal information, in conjunction with your access to and use of our services.

1. Introduction

PrimeLife Hospital respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and medical information you provide while using our website and hospital services.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it in connection with appointment bookings and patient care.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide medical care, manage appointments, and support patient communication.

2.1 Information You Provide

When you book an appointment or communicate with PrimeLife Hospital, you may provide:

  • Your name, email, and phone number
  • Medical history and appointment details
  • Insurance and billing information
  • Patient preferences and special care needs

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our website, we may automatically collect:

  • Device and browser information
  • Usage data from appointment booking and chatbot tools
  • IP address and location data for security and scheduling

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to deliver safe, efficient, and personalized healthcare services.

  • Confirm and manage appointments
  • Communicate appointment reminders and care instructions
  • Provide better patient support and follow-up care
  • Improve our hospital services and website experience

4. Information Sharing

PrimeLife Hospital shares personal information only when necessary to provide your care or when required by law.

4.1 With Your Consent

We may share information with healthcare partners, laboratories, or insurers when you authorize the disclosure.

4.2 Legal and Safety Reasons

We may share information when required by law, to prevent harm, or to comply with government requests.

  • Legal processes and regulatory requirements
  • Medical emergencies and patient safety needs
  • Fraud prevention and security investigations

5. Data Security

We take appropriate measures to protect your patient information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

  • Secure data storage and access controls
  • Encrypted communication for sensitive data
  • Restricted employee access on a need-to-know basis

6. Your Rights

You may request access to your personal information and ask us to correct or update it.

  • Access your patient details
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of outdated personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

7. Changes to This Policy

PrimeLife Hospital may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Updated policies will be posted on this page with the effective date.

Your continued use of our services after the update means you accept the revised policy.
