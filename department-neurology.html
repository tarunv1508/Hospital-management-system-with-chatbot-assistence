Neurological Services

Our neurological services encompass cutting-edge diagnostics, innovative treatments, and comprehensive rehabilitation programs designed to address the full spectrum of brain and nervous system disorders with compassion and expertise.

Stroke Care

24/7 acute stroke intervention including thrombolytic therapy, mechanical thrombectomy, and comprehensive rehabilitation to maximize recovery and prevent recurrence.

Epilepsy Management

Advanced epilepsy care including video EEG monitoring, anti-seizure medication management, surgical evaluation, and lifestyle counseling for seizure control.

Neurophysiology Testing

State-of-the-art neurophysiological testing including EEG, EMG, nerve conduction studies, and evoked potentials for precise diagnosis of neurological conditions.

Movement Disorders

Specialized care for Parkinson's disease, essential tremor, dystonia, and other movement disorders with medication management and advanced therapies.

Headache Clinics

Comprehensive headache management including preventive medications, acute treatment protocols, and trigger identification for migraine and chronic headache disorders.

Cognition Support

Neurocognitive assessment and treatment for dementia, memory disorders, and cognitive impairment with rehabilitation strategies and caregiver support.
